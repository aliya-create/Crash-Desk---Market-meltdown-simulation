Where is the money going? What the reports show
  • Out of big tech and AI chips: the Nasdaq-100 fell 1.39% on Monday, a second straight drop, after Friday’s 2.86% Nasdaq plunge. Okta fell 11.2% and Apple and Palantir slipped after hours.
  • Into cyclicals and the average stock: the equal-weight S&P ETF (RSP) rose 0.76% while the cap-weighted index fell, as oil’s drop on US–Iran diplomacy hopes helped energy-sensitive and consumer names.
  • Into bonds, not the dollar: the 10-year yield slid from about 5.05% on Oct 28 to about 4.90%, and the dollar index fell for three sessions Oct 28–30.
  • Fund flows: SPY saw a $4.6B outflow on Monday while IVV took in $1.4B.
  • Context: midterm elections are today, Nov 3, and the Fed’s hawkish hold has kept yields high.
